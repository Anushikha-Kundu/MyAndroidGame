🚀 Bird Shooter – Rule the Skies!

Get ready for a fast-paced aerial adventure in Bird Shooter, a thrilling 2D arcade game built for Android! Take control of a high-speed aircraft and defend the skies against waves of unpredictable birds. 🐦💥

🎮 What Makes It Fun?

✈️ Simple Controls, Smooth Gameplay
Tap to fly higher or unleash projectiles—easy to learn, hard to master!

🌄 Dynamic Scrolling World
Enjoy seamless backgrounds that keep the action flowing non-stop.

🐤 Smart & Random Enemy Patterns
No two runs feel the same—stay alert!

💥 Real-Time Collision Detection
Quick reflexes are your best weapon.

🏆 High Score Tracker
Beat your best score and challenge yourself every time.

🔊 Immersive Sound Effects + Mute Option
Play it loud or keep it silent—your choice!
